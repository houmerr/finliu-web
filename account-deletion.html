Account and Data Deletion Request

If you would like to delete your FinLiu account and all associated data, you can do so directly within the app's settings menu.

If you have already uninstalled the application and wish to request account deletion, please send an email from your registered email address to: support@finliu.com

Once we receive your request, all your personal financial data, account credentials, and records will be permanently and irreversibly deleted from our servers within 30 days.
